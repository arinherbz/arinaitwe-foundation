Who we are

Community-led change, sustained for generations.

The Arinaitwe Foundation works hand-in-hand with villages and neighbourhoods to design solutions that fit their realities — because change that a community owns is change that lasts.

We focus on what unlocks everything else: education that opens doors, healthcare that arrives in time, and dignified livelihoods that keep people self-reliant.

Read our pillars

Our mission

“To walk alongside underserved communities in East Africa, equipping local leaders and families with the tools, skills, and connections to build healthier, educated, and economically resilient futures.”
  • Rooted in community priorities
  • Partner-led, transparency-first
  • Lifelong capacity building

What we do

Three pillars, one goal: thriving communities.

🎓

Education

Scholarships, learning materials, and teacher support so every child — especially girls — can stay in school and succeed.

✚

Health & Wellbeing

Mobile clinics, maternal-child health outreach, clean water, and health literacy in communities underserved by formal systems.

🏢

Sustainable Enterprise

Village savings groups, skills training, and seed grants that help families build dignified, self-sustaining incomes.

Our programs

Programs that meet people where they are.

Education

Girls Rising Scholarship

School fees, mentorship, and sanitary support for secondary-school girls from low-income households.

Health

Mobile Clinic Outreach

Quarterly pop-up clinics bringing screening, immunisation, and maternal care to rural villages.

Water

Clean Water for All

Protected boreholes and rain-harvesting systems with local water committees to own upkeep.

Enterprise

Saving-to-Living Groups

Village savings and loan circles paired with financial literacy and start-up grants for youth.

Capacity

Teacher Support Network

Learning materials, training days, and stipends that keep quality teachers in rural classrooms.

Leadership

Next-Gen Leaders

A two-year fellowship nurturing young community organisers and changemakers.

Our impact

Just getting started — and honest about it.

Arinaitwe Foundation is in its starting phase. We have no impact numbers to report yet — and we will not invent any. This page will only ever show real, verifiable results, published as our programmes launch and grow.

  • Real results only — no fabricated, projected, or aspirational figures.
  • Updates dated and sourced, so progress can be checked by anyone.
  • Open about what we are still learning, including what has not worked.
